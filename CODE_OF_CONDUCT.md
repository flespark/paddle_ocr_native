# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to making participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on observable technical behavior.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private OCR samples or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security concerns privately through the contact method in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary.
